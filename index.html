The 2027 kit

The new BCN Riders kit is here.

Navy with light-blue BCN wordmarks, Barcelona and Girona on the sleeves, and our route squiggles across every piece. Made in Italy by Obbi. There's one design for everyone: Pro and Sport look identical on the road.

About the club

Barcelona Cycling Network.

Road, gravel and MTB riders in Barcelona who'd rather ride together than alone. The WhatsApp group is where rides get planned, and we keep it full of rides and clear of lurking.

  1. Socks or more, you're in. Anyone who gets at least the Lurker Socks is a BCN Rider for 2027. Pro, Sport or socks, that's the only requirement.
