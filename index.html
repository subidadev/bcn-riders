All measurements are body measurements in centimetres. Go by your measurements, not your usual size in other brands. Pro and Sport jerseys share one chart; the wind vest uses your jersey size.

About the club

Barcelona Cycling Network.

A group for coordinating ride logistics among active cyclists (road, gravel and MTB) who like to ride with others and live in Barcelona. We keep the WhatsApp group full of rides and clear of lurking.

  1. Ride together. Resident of Spain or guest, with at least one ride with someone in the group in the last 30 days.
  2. All levels welcome. Any rider is welcome to join any ride.
  3. Renew for 2027. Order any tier (Pro, Sport or Socks) to keep your place in the chat group.
